CopyDeck

What you send again and again, one tap away on your home screen.

  • Texts and links: tap a name in the widget, the whole message is copied, ready to paste.
  • Photos and files: open them in one tap, or tick a few and share them together.
  • Apps and phone toggles: your shortcuts and switches in the same place.
  • Memes and documents: GIFs and videos too, and optional AI search with your own key finds them by what they show or say.
  • Private: no account, no ads, no server of ours. Everything stays on your phone.
